Tidal portfolio-siting workflow

The user supplies a device specification; its energy and cost characteristics parameterize the two characterization lanes, which feed the portfolio optimization.

Reading the flow: the device specification is the workflow's user input — its energy characteristics parameterize the energy model and its cost characteristics the cost model (color-matched arrows). Swapping the device card (VP Gen5 → ORPC TidGen) re-runs the entire workflow unchanged.
