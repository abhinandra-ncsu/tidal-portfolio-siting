## Note on scope

CBS 1.1.2.4 Electrical Assembly and 1.1.2.7 Power Electrical System are
on-device electrical components (inverters, frequency converters, internal
wiring). They are part of the device, not part of the array-level submarine
cable infrastructure (CBS 1.2.3, handled separately).
